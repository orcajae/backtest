How a backtest runs

  1. Write the rules down

    Entry, exit, size and market, in plain English. I ask until nothing is left to guess.

  2. Run them on real prices

    Every trade logged, costs taken off, nothing seen before its own date.

  3. Report the weak points

    Change one setting and watch the result. A rule that only works at one number is luck.
