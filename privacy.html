Privacy

What the form collects

Your name, your email, the rules and market you describe, the package you pick and where you found me. Nothing else, and no tracking cookies.

The checklist form collects your email and a short code for the link you came from (for example, the Instagram bio). I may email you once about the checklist and never share your address.

Where it goes

The request form is delivered to my inbox by Web3Forms (web3forms.com). Web3Forms also stores each request; its current retention setting keeps requests for up to 3 years. The checklist form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). I use them only to reply to you and to run the backtest you ask for.

Who sees your strategy

Only me. If you take one of the 3 free runs, an anonymized result may be shown on Instagram, with your consent and without your name or exact settings.

Deleting it

Reply to any email from me and ask, and I delete your request and files within 7 days.
